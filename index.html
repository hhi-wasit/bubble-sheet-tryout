Bubble Grader

Print answer sheets, scan them with your phone camera, and grade automatically. No ads, no accounts. Everything stays on your phone.

1. Make answer sheetsChoose question types, set the number of questions, then print an A4 sheet. 2. Scan and gradeEnter the answer keys, photograph each sheet, check the marks, and save the score.

Question types

  • Multiple choice (2 to 5 choices)
  • True / False
  • Multiple select (several correct answers)
  • Matching (each item picks a letter)

Mix up to 3 sections on one sheet, 100 questions in total.

For accurate scans

  1. Print at 100% scale (actual size), never “fit to page”.
  2. Fill bubbles completely with a dark pen or pencil.
  3. Photograph the whole sheet, flat, in even light, with all four black corner squares visible.
  4. Review every orange mark before saving.

Install on your phone

In Chrome on Android, open the menu and choose Install app (or Add to Home screen). It then works offline.
